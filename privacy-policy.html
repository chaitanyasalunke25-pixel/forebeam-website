Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Forebeam ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CAT Preparation Tracker application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information Stored by the App

CAT Preparation Tracker is designed primarily for offline use. Information entered into the App is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your name or display name.
  • Study progress and study-related records.
  • Subjects, chapters, and question-solving records.
  • Daily study entries and session information.
  • Mock-test records and performance information.
  • Reminder and notification schedules.
  • Application preferences and settings.

This information is used to provide the App's study-tracking, progress-monitoring, reminder, and personalization features.

2. Local Storage

The App stores its study and application data locally on your device. The App does not require you to create an account or submit your study records to a Forebeam server in order to use the core features of the App.

Because locally stored information is maintained on your device, you are responsible for maintaining appropriate security of your device and its access credentials.

3. Information the App Does Not Require

The App does not require you to provide personal information such as:

  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Postal address.
  • Precise location information.
  • Date of birth.
  • Government identification information.
  • Banking passwords or UPI PINs.

The App does not require a user account for its core study-tracking functionality.

4. Notifications and Reminders

CAT Preparation Tracker can schedule study reminders and other notifications on your device.

These reminders are scheduled using the device's local notification system. The App may use Android notification and alarm functionality to deliver scheduled reminders, including reminders that are intended to operate when the App is not actively open.

Notification permissions can be controlled through your device's operating-system settings.

5. UPI Tips and Voluntary Contributions

The App may provide an optional feature allowing users to make a voluntary tip or contribution through UPI.

When you choose to use this feature, the App creates a UPI payment request and may open a UPI application installed on your device. The actual payment is processed by the UPI application and the applicable payment service providers.

CAT Preparation Tracker does not request or collect your UPI PIN, banking password, card PIN, or other banking authentication credentials.

Your use of a third-party UPI application is also subject to that provider's own privacy policy and terms of service.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with external applications when you voluntarily use features such as UPI payments.

Such external services operate independently of Forebeam and may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Sharing

Forebeam does not sell your personal information.

The core study-tracking information stored by the App is intended to remain on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to Forebeam for advertising or sale.

Information may be processed by third-party services when you voluntarily interact with those services, such as when initiating a UPI payment through an external UPI application.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of CAT Preparation Tracker does not require advertising or third-party analytics services for its core functionality.

9. Data Deletion

The App provides functionality for resetting study and application records.

You can also manage or remove application data through your device's operating-system settings, subject to the behavior of your device and any applicable backup mechanisms.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to design the App so that its core study information can be maintained locally on the user's device.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should maintain appropriate security on their device, including device authentication where available.

11. Children's Privacy

CAT Preparation Tracker is intended as an educational and examination-preparation application.

The App does not knowingly require children to submit personal information to Forebeam.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of CAT Preparation Tracker, please contact the developer through the official Forebeam website.
